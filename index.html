01 · Learn

An SLA is a management system, not a promise.

A Service Level Agreement converts a commercial relationship into explicit service standards: what must happen, who owns it, how it is measured, what happens when performance deviates, and how the parties review the relationship.

SLA

Contractual commitment

A measurable service level agreed between parties, normally linked to governance and remedies.

SLO

Operational objective

The target used by the operating team. It may be stricter than the contractual SLA to create a safety margin.

KPI

Performance indicator

The metric used to monitor the operation. Not every KPI needs to become a contractual obligation.

Evidence

Measurement proof

Every metric needs a source, period, owner and calculation rule. If it cannot be evidenced, it is difficult to govern.

1 · Define

Scope & ownership

Product content, order acceptance, logistics, returns and incidents. Ambiguous ownership becomes operational friction.

2 · Quantify

Targets & thresholds

Time, availability, return rate and customer rating become explicit thresholds with a clear “higher/lower is better” rule.

3 · Govern

Remedies & review

Service credits, bonuses, monitoring cadence, alerts and change control turn the SLA into an active governance mechanism.

Teaching principle. A strong SLA combines commercial logic, operational accountability, data evidence and customer impact. A target without an owner or a measurement source is not yet a usable SLA.
